## Leakage

This sonification comes from the Thames Water leakage issues. 

The data, hardcoded into a script, is taken from the Thames Water Annual Performance 24-25 report. 

The sonification uses two effects to alter each second of audio after a one second clean introduction. 

The first effect is time stretching to alter each one second sample before it returns to the intro to end.

The second alters pitch and makes the sound like it is underwater.

The intention is to develop a more accessible musification / sonification than using abstract entities. 

The music is Handel's Water Music that referenced the Thames, so seemed apt from tow perspectives. First it is about the region's main river and secondly is a well-known track. This version was recorded in 1946 and is on the Internet Archive (https://archive.org/details/mx-279-handel-water-music-suite). It is well known enough that I feel it can be played and a general audience will recognise the tune before it is affected by the data.
